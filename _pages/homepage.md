Organizational change is constant.
Many organizations respond by optimizing. Moving faster, adding tools, improving performance.

But optimization doesn’t create direction.
Conscious choice does.

I explore organizational change through the lens of leadership and Data & Analytics.
Not to provide more answers, but to improve how decisions are made when things get complicated.

On this site, I share my work and thinking around Data & Analytics, leadership, and the lessons I pick up along the way.
